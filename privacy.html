Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Defy ("the App", "we") handles your information.

Data stored on your device

Your quiz answers, quit date, streaks, check-ins and relapse logs are stored locally on your device. Deleting the App (or using "Reset all data" in Settings) removes this data from your device.

Data we receive

  • Anonymous usage data. The App may send anonymous check-in and event data (e.g. craving intensity, a "craving beaten" event) tied to a randomly generated identifier. This identifier does not contain your name, email, or contacts, and we cannot use it to identify you.
  • Purchase information. Subscriptions are processed by Apple and by RevenueCat, our subscription-management provider. We receive anonymized subscription status (e.g. active/expired). We never see your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show third-party ads.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, or location.

Health disclaimer

Defy supports habit change. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Information about nicotine withdrawal and recovery timelines is general in nature. Consult a healthcare professional for medical concerns.

Children

The App is intended for users aged 18 and older.

Data retention & deletion

Anonymous server-side records are kept only for product analytics. To request deletion of data associated with your random identifier, contact us with the identifier shown in the App.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
